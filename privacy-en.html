DrillBall Privacy Policy

Developer display name / brand: Diverse.Studio
App: DrillBall / 굴착볼 (Android)
Effective date: 2026-10-06

1. Services currently available

DrillBall is a mineral-mining game. Free play does not require login or an internet connection; progress is saved on your device. The current development build uses Google's official test ads.

Developer accounts, login, cloud saves, paid product sales, server purchase verification and a public advertising-reward verification server are not currently operated. Included code or libraries do not mean these services are live. Before activation, we will update this policy to reflect actual processing and provide required notices.

2. Information stored on your device

The game stores stage progress, coins, mining records, improvements, settings, resume state, reward and tool transaction records, and technical diagnostic records locally. Sending progress to a Diverse.Studio production server is not enabled. We have not connected an additional developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service. This does not mean Google SDKs process no information.

The current app does not use Android automatic app backup. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can permanently remove local progress; a developer server cannot restore it.

3. Google advertising SDK and privacy choices

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses, advertising and app identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. IP addresses can indicate approximate location. Data is encrypted in transit. Test ads can also communicate with Google.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to refresh consent information and requests ads only when the SDK permits it. When the SDK requires privacy options, its form can be opened from app settings. Choices depend on region and message configuration. The production app's European consent message is being prepared; we do not claim it is published.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about apps using its services. Android advertising privacy settings are also available. Changing consent does not cancel previously confirmed game rewards.

4. Google Play library

The app includes Google Play Billing and may connect to Play at startup to query products or ownership status. Actual purchases, coin-pack sales and developer-account linking are currently disabled. Diverse.Studio does not provide a feature for entering payment-card details. Google's policies govern Google Play processing. QA purchase simulations are not real payments.

5. Support inquiries and this website

If you send an inquiry, your supplied email address and information reach the person handling it. Send only what is necessary. Do not send passwords, full purchase tokens, identity documents or payment-card details.

Once the inquiry purpose is fulfilled, the person handling it removes unnecessary personal information, retaining only what applicable obligations require. The person handling inquiries reviews and cleans up received and sent emails, attachments and separately saved copies. We do not promise automatic deletion or immediate complete erasure.

The public email uses Gmail. Google processes email transmission and storage under its Privacy Policy, separately from Diverse.Studio's handling of inquiries. The email link opens your mail application; this website does not collect your inquiry.

This static website has no developer-added ads, analytics or tracking scripts, login, inquiry forms or first-party cookies. This website is hosted on free GitHub Pages. GitHub Pages records and stores visitor IP addresses for security, whether or not visitors are logged in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Hosting access records and the developer's local game storage are separate.

6. Deletion, choices and requests

Delete local game information by clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling. This erases progress. No developer-server user account is currently created, and there is no server-account deletion portal.

Use Google's privacy tools and Android advertising settings to manage information Google processes and advertising identifiers. Requests about inquiry information held by Diverse.Studio, including access, correction or deletion, may be sent to the contact below and handled under applicable law and the information actually held. The developer cannot remotely access or delete device-local game information.

Public contact email: diversestudio.support@gmail.com
Email display name: Diverse.Studio Support

7. Intended audience

DrillBall is a general puzzle game intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and adults; it is not designed primarily for children under 13. This does not determine its content rating or the age of privacy consent in every country. The current app does not collect dates of birth or provide an age-verification screen. We do not claim age-specific advertising handling is already implemented; required SDK and consent settings for intended countries will be reviewed separately before production advertising.

8. Processing locations and policy changes

Providers such as Google may process information outside your country under their policies and applicable law. We do not specify a location or retention period for a developer server that is not operated.

When features or processing change, we will update this page and its effective date and provide in-app notice or consent procedures where required.
