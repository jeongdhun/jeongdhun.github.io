Brick Volt Privacy Policy

Developer display name / brand: Diverse.Studio
App: Brick Volt / 브릭볼트 (Android)
Package: com.diversestudio.brickvolt
Effective date: 2026-10-25

1. The game currently provided

Brick Volt is a casual brick-breaking puzzle intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and adults. Play does not require a developer account or login, and progress is stored on your device. Paid purchases and cloud saves are not currently offered.

The gameplay screens do not ask you to enter a name, email address, date of birth or login details. We do not operate a separate analytics service.

The current closed-test version includes no advertising or consent SDK. The game makes no ad requests and does not send game progress outside your device.

2. Progress stored on your device

The game stores stage progress and clears, starting-ball growth, resume state, endless-mode records, sound settings and cosmetic selections on your device. It does not provide a feature that uploads this progress to a Diverse.Studio production server.

Clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling can cause loss of local progress. We do not provide a service to restore or delete device-local progress remotely. Clearing device storage does not also delete support emails already sent or information processed by external websites.

3. External browser and email links

Selecting a privacy-policy or support link in the game's settings opens a webpage in your external browser. Email links open your email application. Opening a webpage or sending email can involve information handling by those external services, separately from the game's local storage.

4. Support email and this website

If you contact support by email, the address, message and attachments you send reach the person handling your inquiry so it can be reviewed and answered. Send only what is necessary. The public support mailbox uses Gmail, whose service processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

This support page provides a link that opens your email application. It has no separate contact form, login, developer-added analytics or tracking scripts, or first-party cookies. When hosted on GitHub Pages, the hosting provider can process connection information such as IP addresses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Retention, deletion requests and contact

We use inquiry information to answer your request and carry out necessary follow-up. Once that work is complete and the information is no longer needed, we delete it. Information needed to meet legal obligations or handle disputes is kept only to the extent and for the period needed.

You can use the contact below to request access to, correction of or deletion of inquiry information. We check the information we hold and confirm the requester before handling the request and replying with the result. For information held by Google, Gmail or GitHub, please consult the relevant provider's retention and deletion policies and procedures.

6. Intended audience and policy changes

Brick Volt is intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and adults, and is not primarily designed for children under 13.

If advertising is introduced or information handling changes, we update this page and its effective date and provide any necessary notices.
