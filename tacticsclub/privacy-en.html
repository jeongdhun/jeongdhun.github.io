작전의 시간 Privacy Policy

App: 작전의 시간 (Android)
Developer display name: Diverse.Studio
Effective date: October 27, 2026

1. Scope and app data handling

작전의 시간 is an offline strategy game against AI clubs. It has no registration, sign-in, online multiplayer, developer server, or cloud saving. The app does not transmit, collect, or share game or user information with the developer or third parties.

The app does not integrate advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, tracking, or remote crash reporting services or their SDKs. It does not request internet permission or access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or similar information unnecessary for gameplay.

2. Information stored on your device

To continue games and recover progress, the app stores club and roster data, player names you choose, player growth and match records, season and series progress, in-game currency, and settings in its app-specific device storage. Local recovery copies may also remain on the same device to handle save failures or format changes. You do not need to use real names or contact details for player names.

This information is used only on your device and is not remotely accessible to the developer. Android automatic app backup is disabled. There is no device synchronization or recovery from a developer server.

3. Retention and deleting local information

Local information remains in the app's storage. Use Android Settings → Apps → 작전의 시간 → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app, to delete local saves and recovery copies. Menu names vary by device. This removes progress, which the developer cannot restore.

There is no developer server account to delete. Uninstalling the app does not delete support emails you have separately sent.

4. Support emails you choose to send

If you contact us, we receive your sender email address, display name, message, and any files you choose to attach. We use this information as needed to respond, investigate the issue, and manage the inquiry history. We do not use it for advertising or user tracking or post it publicly. You can play without contacting us.

Access is limited to those handling the inquiry. Do not send passwords, identity documents, financial information, or unnecessary personal information. We review and delete inquiry emails and separately saved copies when no longer needed after resolving the inquiry, except where retention is legally required. We do not promise automatic deletion or immediate erasure of every copy.

Our support mailbox uses Gmail. Google's processing for email transmission and storage is described in the Google Privacy Policy. This does not mean the game app transmits game data.

5. This support website

These static support pages contain no developer-added advertising, analytics or tracking scripts, contact forms, sign-in, or cookies. Email links open your email application.

The website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, whether or not they are signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. Google and GitHub may process information outside your country of residence under their policies and applicable law.

6. Your choices and requests

You can delete device information through Android storage settings. To request access, correction, or deletion of inquiry information we hold, contact the address below. Requests are handled according to information actually held and applicable law. We cannot remotely access or delete local game information on your device.

7. Intended audience and changes

The game is intended for teenagers aged 13 and above and adults; it is not designed primarily for children under 13. It does not collect dates of birth or verify age. This does not determine any country's age of privacy consent or Google Play content rating. All game functions are provided in Korean.

If game features or information handling change, we will update this policy and its effective date, provide appropriate notice, and follow legally required procedures.
