Lootember: Idle RPG — Privacy Policy
Publisher: Diverse.Studio
Package: com.diversestudio.lootember
Effective date: 2026-10-19
Applies to the current closed-test version of Lootember: Idle RPG.

Game data and accounts
This test version has no account, login or developer-operated cloud service. Progress such as level, gold, equipment, skills and upgrades is stored locally in the app's private storage. Diverse.Studio does not receive this game progress.

Data collection and sharing
Our game code does not collect or transmit personal information, device identifiers or gameplay analytics. No advertising, analytics, external crash-reporting or in-app billing SDK has been added to the project.

Advertising and purchases
This test build has no ads, advertising-ID access or in-app purchases. Gold is earned through gameplay and used for in-game equipment upgrades. It is not a payment balance.

Storage, retention and deletion
The app provides local saves and no developer-operated cloud backup or synchronization. Android automatic app backup is configured as disabled for this build. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling normally deletes the local save. Device/operating-system migration or platform services may have their own behavior and policies. An update installed over the same signed app is intended to preserve local progress.

Platform services
Android, Google Play and your device manufacturer may process information under their own policies independently of this offline game. We do not provide an analytics or crash-upload service inside this build.

Contacting support
If you choose to email diversestudio.support@gmail.com, you provide your email address and the contents of your message outside the game. We use those details to respond to your request, retain them only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations, and do not use them for advertising. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the same address.

Children and target audience
This closed-test build is intended for people aged 13 and older, as confirmed by the publisher. The game does not create user profiles or include advertising in this build. Google Play content-rating answers and the resulting rating are handled separately.

This policy website and email provider
This policy is hosted on the existing Diverse.Studio GitHub Pages site. No developer-added advertising, analytics, tracking scripts, login, inquiry forms or first-party cookies are included in this policy page. GitHub Pages records and stores visitor IP addresses for security, independently of game-local storage. GitHub's privacy statement: GitHub Privacy Statement
Support email uses Gmail. Google processes email transmission and storage under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy . This is separate from Diverse.Studio's handling of support correspondence. Send only information needed for support; do not send passwords, identity documents or payment-card details.

Opening this policy from the app
The privacy-policy button opens a fixed public HTTPS page in your external browser. It does not add game progress, account identifiers or other user data to the URL. Your browser and the website host handle the page request under their own policies.

Changes
We will update this policy before introducing a change that affects the practices described here.
