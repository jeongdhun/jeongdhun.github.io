Mix & Fill Privacy Policy

Developer display name / brand: Diverse.Studio
App: Mix & Fill (Android)
Google Play package: com.diversestudio.mixandfill
Effective date: 2026-10-10

1. Scope and current game

This policy applies to Mix & Fill, a real-time paint-routing and color-mixing puzzle game. It describes the current P0.5 game and first closed-test build. Playing the game does not require login, an account, or an internet connection.

The current app has no advertising, analytics, remote crash-reporting, or payment SDKs. It does not operate a developer account service, cloud saves, or a gameplay server. Test hint and continue buttons do not show real ads or transmit ad-related information. Policies for other Diverse.Studio games, including DrillBall, do not describe Mix & Fill's data practices.

2. Information handled in the game

The app saves the current regular level, unlocked level, sound and haptic preferences, and the selected playtest and its rhythm setting on your device. This information is used only to restore your game progress and preferences.

These saves are kept in the app's local storage managed by Android. They are not uploaded to Diverse.Studio or shared with advertising or analytics providers. The app does not use Android automatic app backup and does not provide cloud synchronization.

Through gameplay, Diverse.Studio does not request or receive your name, email address, location, contacts, advertising identifier, photos, microphone recordings, or payment details. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions. Its vibration permission is used for optional game haptics.

3. Retention, deletion, and security of local saves

Local progress and settings remain on your device until they are changed by the game or removed. You can delete them by clearing Mix & Fill's app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. This also removes your saved progress. Diverse.Studio cannot remotely read, delete, or restore these device-local saves.

The app uses Android's app-specific storage access controls. We do not claim that saves are independently encrypted or that a compromised device cannot expose them. There is no developer-server game account to delete.

4. Support and privacy inquiries

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the message, screenshots, or other information you supply. We use this information to respond to your inquiry or address the issue you report. Send only information needed for your request; do not send passwords, signing keys, identity documents, or payment-card details.

Once the inquiry's purpose is fulfilled, the person handling it reviews and removes unnecessary personal information from received and sent emails, attachments, and separately saved copies. Information may be retained where required by applicable obligations. We do not promise automatic deletion or immediate complete erasure.

You may request access, correction, or deletion of inquiry information we hold at the contact below. Requests are handled according to applicable law and the information actually held. The contact link opens your mail application; this website has no inquiry form.

Privacy and support contact: diversestudio.support@gmail.com
Email display name: Diverse.Studio Support

5. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell or share device-local game saves with third parties. The public support email uses Gmail, so Google handles email transmission and storage separately under Google's Privacy Policy. Information voluntarily sent by email is separate from the game's local saves.

Google Play and your device platform may process information when you use their own installation, review, or other services under their policies. This is separate from information collected by Mix & Fill; the game does not integrate advertising or analytics SDKs.

6. This privacy-policy website

This static website has no developer-added ads, analytics or tracking scripts, login, forms, or first-party cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in. Hosting access records are separate from the app's local game saves. See GitHub Pages' data-collection notice and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Children and privacy questions

The current game does not request a date of birth or provide account registration, advertising tracking, or a feature for submitting personal information during gameplay. A parent or guardian may contact us with privacy questions or requests about information voluntarily supplied in an inquiry. This statement does not determine the game's store content rating or target-age selection.

8. Processing locations

Game saves stay on your device. Providers such as Google for support email and GitHub for this website may process information outside your country under their own policies and applicable law. We do not specify a location or retention period for a developer gameplay server that is not operated.

9. Changes to this policy

If features or data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date to describe the actual processing, and provide notices or consent procedures where required. The absence of advertising and analytics described here applies to the current implementation.
